## Analysis

_One-off midday run (11:54 ET). Spot 7,173._

### Regime: negative gamma
Net GEX is **−$81B per 1% move**, deep in negative territory. Put gamma (−$143B) outweighs call gamma (+$62B) by more than 2 to 1, and the put/call open-interest ratio is 1.54. Dealers are positioned to **amplify** moves: they sell into weakness and buy into strength. Expect trend-following intraday action, wider ranges, and less reliable mean reversion than in a positive-gamma tape. The gamma flip sits at **7,476**, **+304 pts (+4.2%)** above spot, far enough away that a regime change today is very unlikely.

**Confluences**
- **7,200** is both the 0DTE call wall and a large OI strike (18k calls, 46k puts). It sits just inside the expected-move high, so it's the first real test for upside.
- **7,150** (0DTE put wall, 15.5k calls and 23k puts) is the nearest support. The **7,122** expected-move low lines up with the 7,125 strike just below it.
- **7,000** is by far the largest gamma strike on the board, about 8× the next biggest. It's the downside magnet and the most likely place for a hard stop if selling accelerates.

### Scenarios
- **Holds 7,150–7,200 (base case):** 0DTE gamma is concentrated in 7,140–7,190, so price may pin and chop in this band into the close, especially in the last hour as 0DTE gamma peaks.
- **Breaks above 7,200:** the profile shows negative gamma easing fast as price rises (−$68B at 7,245, −$48B at 7,315). Moves higher still get amplified, but a squeeze would likely stall at the **7,250 / 7,300** OI clusters, well short of the 7,400 call wall.
- **Loses 7,150 / 7,122:** this is the dangerous direction. Negative gamma *deepens* on the way down, to about −$89B near 7,000–7,030. Dealer hedging adds to selling, which opens a run toward **7,100**, then **7,050**, and eventually the **7,000** put wall. Expect a reaction there, where gamma bottoms out.
- **Overall skew:** risk is lopsided to the downside. Upside moves lose dealer fuel as they go, while downside moves gain it.
